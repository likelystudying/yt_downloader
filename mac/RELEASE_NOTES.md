**YT Downloader for Mac**: download YouTube videos (MP4) and audio (MP3) from Chrome, without Terminal.

**Install:** unzip, drag **YT Downloader** into Applications, open it (first time: System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Open Anyway), then follow the message to add the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
Full steps: [mac/README.md](https://github.com/likelystudying/yt_downloader/blob/mac-v1.0.0/mac/README.md).

Built for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer), macOS 12 or later.
